About this calculator

This calculator estimates your monthly mortgage payment and the other costs of owning a home, such as property tax, insurance, PMI, HOA dues and upkeep. You can also add an extra monthly payment to see how much sooner the loan is paid off. It is designed for fixed-rate loans in the U.S.

How a mortgage works

A mortgage is a loan secured by the home. Each payment covers interest, the price of borrowing, and principal, the amount you borrowed. Early payments are mostly interest, and the principal share grows over time, which the schedule above shows.

Key inputs

Down payment: cash you pay up front. Under 20% usually means paying PMI. Loan term: 15 and 30 years are common, and shorter terms carry lower rates but higher payments. Interest rate: the yearly rate, charged monthly at one twelfth.

Ongoing and one-time costs

Property tax, insurance, HOA dues and maintenance continue after the loan is paid. Closing costs, moving and repairs are one-time and are not included in the estimate.

Paying off early

Extra payments go straight to principal, cutting total interest and shortening the loan. Check your loan for prepayment penalties and compare the savings with what that money could earn elsewhere.

Estimates only. Not a loan offer or financial advice.
